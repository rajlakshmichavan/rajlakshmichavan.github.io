Listen to the fans
May 2021

On May 10, 2021, NCT Dream came back with a full studio album for the first time in 4 years. With this comeback, they returned from the brink of disbandment, added Mark back to the lineup, surpassed 1.7 million sales in pre-orders, and became just the third artist — and the only one from the fourth generation of idols — to officially sell a million copies in first-week sales, after BTS and Seventeen.

NCT Dream is the youngest sub-unit of Neo Culture Technology, SM Entertainment's youngest k-pop boy group. NCT Dream debuted on August 25, 2016 with the single “Chewing Gum” with an average member age of 15.6 years old. The unit had a unique “graduation” concept: every member, upon turning 20, would leave the group, since Dream was promoted as the NCT unit representing teenagers and their youth.

After Mark graduated in 2018, four of the remaining six members were set to graduate in 2020, leaving only Chenle and Jisung. At their first and supposedly final concert on November 17, 2019 in Seoul, members gave emotional goodbye speeches. Maknae Jisung even spoke about how that could be his last concert ever.

After the concert's fancams went viral, fans took to Twitter to express their wishes about Dream staying together and abolishing the graduation system. Their unity and persistence showed in the numbers. Finally, in 2020, SM Entertainment announced the abolishment of the graduation system. “7Dream” began trending globally on Twitter after the announcement.

The latest comeback, “Hot Sauce,” has broken records in Korea and globally. The title track was the fastest song to reach 10,000 likes on MelOn, surpassing soloist IU. The album topped iTunes charts in 37 countries.

The most important lesson to pick up from this case study is the fact that listening to what the fans want — and not giving fans what the company thinks they want — is the best recipe for success. Dream has been luckier than most. Of course, while the fans wanted the group to stay together because of the close bond between the members, the company also saw a massive potential profit opportunity. It just proves that it is what the fans want that pays.
