The Fun Part, at 8:10
Before 2022
Here, I started college and found my groove. Started studying what I liked, met professors who encouraged my curiosity (and delusions), and met my people. The ones who made 20 hour workdays feel like all play and no work, six months at a time. This was also the time I co-founded the 8:10 with Devangee Halder - GIPE's longest running undergraduate student initiative and college newsletter, that has published 200+ articles till date. All of the articles given below are articles I have written for the 8:10. I wrote a lot more under the moniker 'The Editorial Team,' and edited countless more, often burning the midnight oil with Devangee on call and our professors online to give the final yes. That was the time of no ChatGPT or Claude, just you and a blank Google Doc standing between the requirements we set for ourselves. 12 articles, across 4 sections, precisely on the 30th of every month at 8:10 am.
